Our Story

About Ahpu

Built by restaurateurs, for restaurateurs. Ahpu is Nepal's most complete restaurant management platform.

Our Vision

Transforming How Nepal's Restaurants Operate

We believe every restaurant in Nepal deserves access to world-class technology — not just the big chains. Ahpu was born from a simple idea: give every restaurant owner the tools to run a smarter, more profitable business.

Our vision is to be the operating system for Nepal's hospitality industry — the one platform that handles billing, kitchen, inventory, marketing, and analytics under one roof.

We combine deep local knowledge (Nepali fiscal year, IRD compliance, Fonepay, eSewa) with modern software design to deliver something that just works, every day.

Why Ahpu?

Nepal's restaurant industry was underserved by generic billing software that didn't understand local needs — BS calendar, IRD reporting, Fonepay QR, and the unique workflows of Nepali dining culture.

We built Ahpu to fill that gap — a solution that speaks your language, follows your rules, and grows with your business.

Our Mission

To empower every restaurant, cafe, and hospitality business in Nepal with tools that reduce manual work, eliminate billing errors, and provide the clarity needed to make confident business decisions.

We do this by listening to our clients constantly, shipping improvements fast, and keeping our software reliable every single day.

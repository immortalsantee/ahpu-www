Quick help

Try these first.

Most day-to-day questions can be answered in a few seconds, without leaving your Ahpu screen.

Ask Ahpu AI

Press Ctrl + / (⌘ + / on Mac) and ask in English or Nepali. Staff on the mobile app can use AI Support.

Find any page fast

Press Ctrl + K to search pages, reports and accounting. Your last 10 pages show first.

Printer not printing?

Check the printer is on the same network and the Printer Service Bridge is running. The Print-Fail Log shows what didn't print.

Fonepay payment missing?

Open the order and use Reconcile to fetch the payment. If the amount doesn't match the bill, Ahpu won't create the bill automatically.

Backups

Database backups run automatically. Super admins can check the backup log and server information under Statutory Reports.

Need us to look in?

A super admin can create a temporary access link, so our team can help without you sharing a password.

Frequently asked questions

Ask on WhatsApp
How do I get started with Ahpu?
Message or call us and we'll set up a demo with your own menu. After that, we load your items, tables, printers and users and train your staff. Setup usually takes 1–2 days.
Does Ahpu work with Fonepay and eSewa?
Yes. Each order gets its own Fonepay dynamic QR, and the bill is created automatically when the right amount arrives. eSewa, cash, card, bank, credit, customer wallet and split payments are supported too.
Is Ahpu ready for Nepal's IRD rules?
Yes. Ahpu supports IRD-certified billing, the abbreviated tax invoice, sales register and sales return books, and the VAT return in the new IRD portal layout. It handles 13% VAT correctly on mixed bills and uses the Nepali fiscal year and BS calendar.
Does it include accounting?
Yes. Ahpu has full double-entry accounting. Bills, payments, returns and voids post to the journal automatically. You get ledgers, a trial balance, a balance sheet, an income statement, opening balances and fiscal year close.
What is Ahpu AI and is it safe?
Ahpu AI is an assistant built into the app. It can open pages, save expenses, add customers and items, and draft bills, purchases and journal entries. It always shows you a preview before saving, uses the same checks as the normal forms, and every question and answer is logged.
Can I use Ahpu on a tablet or phone?
Yes. The POS and the active order screens work on desktop, tablet and phone. There's also an Android waiter app, a KDS app for the kitchen and the Ahpu mobile app for items and stock.
What if the internet goes down?
Ahpu runs on your local network. Fonts and scripts load from your own server, so you can keep taking orders and billing when the internet is slow. Online features like Fonepay and SMS will pick up again when the connection comes back.
What happens to my data if something goes wrong?
Database backups run automatically and are logged. The activity log keeps a full record of who did what, so you can always trace a change.
Do you train our staff?
Yes. We give hands-on training to cashiers, waiters, kitchen staff and managers so everyone is confident from the first day of service.

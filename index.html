Point of sale

Built for the
Friday-night rush.

Fast with a touchscreen, a mouse or a keyboard. Staff can take an order, send it to the kitchen and print the bill without leaving the screen.

  • Active order popup on desktop, tablet and phone. Add items, change quantities and add notes.
  • Split payments with a one-tap Fill button for the remaining amount.
  • Discount limits checked to the paisa, plus combos and on-the-house orders.
  • Tax invoice, abbreviated tax invoice or estimate. Void or return a bill, with or without a refund.
  • Edit lock so two staff can never change the same order at once.
  • Ctrl + K command palette, pinned menu links and a lock screen with a live clock.

Accounting

Your accountant
will love this part.

Full double-entry accounting sits inside your POS. There's nothing to export and nothing to type twice. Your books follow the Nepali fiscal year.

  • Real-time posting for bills, customer payments, sales returns and voids.
  • Trial balance tree, balance sheet, income statement and ledgers with brought-forward balances.
  • Fiscal years (FY 083/84) with opening balances and carry-forward, plus close and lock.
  • Customer & vendor ledgers, aging reports and statements you can download.
  • VAT return in the new IRD portal layout, with a journal audit log and AI-drafted entries.

Payments

Scan, pay, done.
The bill makes itself.

Each order gets its own Fonepay dynamic QR. When the right amount arrives, Ahpu creates the bill on its own. If the amount doesn't match, it stops and tells you.

  • QR linked to the order with a signed token, so a payment can't land on the wrong table.
  • Reconcile and view transactions right inside the order popup.
  • Customer display on a second monitor shows the order and the QR.
